06 / Safety by design

Safety is a product feature.

The assistant is designed to make uncertainty visible—and to know when the next step belongs with a human expert.

The promise is not certainty.
It is a more defensible first pass.
Four boundaries

Guardrails are part of the response contract, not a disclaimer added at the end.

01
Information
not advice
The interface repeatedly frames outputs as legal information and triage—not a legal conclusion.
02
Abstain when
evidence is thin
Insufficient or incomplete provenance lowers confidence and surfaces the limitation instead of inventing guidance.
03
TKDL pointer
not open access
A public WIPO pointer is kept distinct from restricted database access or a claimed prior-art search.
04
Secure by
workflow
Authentication, admin roles, validation, rate limiting, backend-only secrets, and audit events protect the evidence trail.
Boundary statement

IP-SAKTI Sahayak does not provide legal advice, a patentability opinion, a licensing decision, or unrestricted access to proprietary knowledge databases.
