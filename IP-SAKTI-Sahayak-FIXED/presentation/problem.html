01 / The problem

The problem is not a lack of information.

It is the distance between a formulation idea and the evidence needed to act responsibly.

One product question can hide four different decision tracks.
01
IP position
What is new, what is already known, and which claim or process actually needs protection?
02
Regulatory path
Does the formulation sit within a classical, proprietary, new-drug, food, phytopharmaceutical, or cosmetic route?
03
ABS obligations
Could the use of an Indian biological resource or associated knowledge trigger a biodiversity or access-and-benefit-sharing question?
04
Prior art / TKDL
Is there a public traditional-knowledge pointer, and where must a specialist verify novelty or database access?
