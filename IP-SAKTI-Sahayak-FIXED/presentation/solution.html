03 / The solution

A guided evidence desk.

IP-SAKTI Sahayak turns a broad commercialization question into a sequence of smaller, auditable decisions.

Not a chatbot that guesses. A workflow that shows what it knows, where it came from, and when to ask a human.
One question. Five moves.

Multilingual input, structured routing, source-grounded output.

01

Ask

Describe the formulation, claim, ingredient, or decision ahead.

02

Route

Separate IP, regulatory, ABS, and prior-art signals.

03

Classify

Use targeted questions to identify the formulation category.

04

Retrieve

Match the question to curated authoritative passages.

05

Explain

Return citations, confidence, limitations, and escalation.

Output one / evidence trailExact source metadata, relevant passage, and confidence level.
Output two / human handoffA structured review request when the decision needs qualified expertise.
